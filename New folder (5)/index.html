    FAQ

    What is included in the service?

    I set up your core email flows, or redesign the ones you already have, and add more as your business needs them. I also design and write your regular campaigns and test things like subject lines and layouts to improve results. Copy and design come together, so you can focus on growing your business.

    How quickly will we see results?

    It depends on your industry, your list, and how much email marketing you’ve done before. Brands new to regular email may see changes sooner, but steady growth comes over time as flows, campaigns and testing build on each other.

    Do you have any lock ins?

    No contracts. I recommend working together for at least two months, because email results build over time and the real impact usually shows after that. After that, you continue only if you’re getting real value.

    What makes you different from other email marketers?

    I plan the copy and design together, so every email has one clear goal. You work directly with me, with no outsourcing, and I focus on emails that drive real sales, not vanity metrics.

    How many email designs do I get?

    It depends on the month and what’s needed, such as holidays, flow redesigns and the number of campaigns. We agree on the number together before we start.

    Who will manage our email platform account?

    I will manage your email platform account myself, so you always work directly with me, with no outsourcing and no hand offs.

    How can we approve the emails before send?

    I share your emails in Figma, so you can view each design through a link, leave comments right on it, and ask for changes. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

    Will I be working directly with you?

    Yes. You’ll work directly with me by email, and every message goes straight to me, with no account managers in between.
